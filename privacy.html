Privacy policy

tapo-uploader is a personal tool used only by its author. It is not distributed to or used by other people.

What the app accesses

With the author's explicit consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API (scope youtube) on the author's own Google account to upload videos, create playlists and add videos to them.

What is stored

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the author's own computer or server. Videos are recorded locally and deleted after a successful upload.

Sharing

No data is collected from third parties, sold, shared or sent anywhere other than YouTube, and only to the author's own channel.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
